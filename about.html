The Unconventional Path

Building systems that connect people is the best way to describe what I do. It might start with a piece of tech, an SOP, an unwritten process, or something that lives in an execs head. It ends with a build that intersects accessibility and utility. And the value comes from the labor you save, the focus and morale you restore for colleagues and employees, and the money you make with organized pipeline management and automated follow-ups. It's much deeper than that, but I was told to pretend like I was on an elevator in that one class in business school years ago. And I think I could say all of that on an elevator if we went up a dozen floors or so.
